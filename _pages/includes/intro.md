# Welcome to the personal website of Bing Xu!

Hello! This is the personal website of **Bing Xu**.

Bing Xu is a PhD researcher who is trying to survive, understand, and hopefully enjoy academic life. His research focuses on **Geographic Information Science**, especially landslide detection, disaster event localization, and spatiotemporal inference. More specifically, he works with social media posts, remote sensing images, rainfall data, terrain information, and other geospatial features to figure out where and when landslides actually happened. 

His current research aims to fuse **multimodal social media information** with **geographic knowledge** to improve situational awareness in disaster response.
